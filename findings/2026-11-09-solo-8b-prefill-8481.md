# Solo-8B prefill at 8,481 tokens = 1270.29 tok/s; deeper arms unmeasurable on 8 GB

- **Date:** 2026-11-09 (certified, scoped)
- **Platform:** Fortress — see `platforms/fortress-provisional.md`
  (2026-11-08 fingerprint)
- **Source record:** FORTRESS-TUNING.md **F-63**; experiment R16-T5
  (on-box results `r16_t5.json`)

## Question

What is the solo 8B's prefill rate as prompts deepen toward
the context ceiling?

## Method

qwen3:8b solo on the RTX 4000, prefill arms at ~8.5k,
~17k, and ~34k prompt tokens.

## Measured result

- The ~8.5k arm is valid: **prompt 1270.29 tok/s at 8,481
  tokens** (ctx 16384, f16 KV).
- The ~17k arm (ctx 20480, f16) and the ~34k arm
  (ctx 40960, q8_0 KV) both **failed to load** — no prefill
  rates exist for them, and none are certified. Their
  failure is consistent with F-59's footprint ceiling.

## Verdict

**CERTIFIED, scoped to the loadable arm only.** Prefill at
8,481 tokens is 1270.29 tok/s; deeper prefill on this model
is bounded by the same 8 GB footprint ceiling as decode
context (F-59), not by compute.
