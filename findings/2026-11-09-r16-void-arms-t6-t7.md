# R16 void arms — T6 (draft variety) and T7 (Fortress VL baseline): no measurement exists

- **Date:** 2026-11-09 (void — recorded, not certified)
- **Platform:** Fortress — see `platforms/fortress-provisional.md`
  (2026-11-08 fingerprint)
- **Source record:** FORTRESS-TUNING.md, "R16 items NOT
  certified" (Nov 9, 2026 review); experiments R16-T6 and
  R16-T7 (on-box results `r16_t6.json`, `r16_t7.json`)

## What was attempted

- **T6 — draft variety:** whether a larger draft (3B / 4B)
  can beat the 0.6b draft on the 8B target, same-card.
- **T7 — Fortress VL baseline:** qwen2.5vl:3b generation on
  both cards via pinned throwaway Ollama instances.

## What happened

- **T6 — VOID as a measurement.** All three arms failed at
  server start from draft-load VRAM OOM, verified in the
  on-box server logs. Zero rates exist; the question
  remains open. (Its load-level observation — same-card
  drafting at ctx 8192 is limited to sub-1B drafts — is
  preserved inside F-58.)
- **T7 — VOID, harness failure.** Both pinned Ollama
  instances returned HTTP 500 because the throwaway store
  used *symlinked manifest files*, which Ollama 0.40.14
  rejects ("bad manifest — manifest symlink target is not
  a sha256 blob") for every model in the store. No
  generation ever ran; the Fortress VL baseline remained
  unmeasured at the close of R16. The re-run with a copied
  (dereferenced-manifest) store produced the baseline in
  R17 — see F-69.

## Verdict

**VOID — published as void.** No numbers are claimed from
either arm. They are filed so the record shows the
questions were asked, why they produced nothing, and where
the surviving parts of each question were answered (F-58,
F-69).
