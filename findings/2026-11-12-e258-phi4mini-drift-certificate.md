# phi4-mini drift certificate: CERTIFIED — with the hook axis filed as a range, not a point

- **Date:** 2026-11-12 (certified, main-chat ruling)
- **Platform:** Mint — see `platforms/mint.md`
- **Source record:** TUNING #278 (ruling on the E258
  certificate, experiment record #271), corroborated by
  #275 (E260)

## Question

Has phi4-mini's metadata behavior drifted since its
restoration certificate — re-measured on the cert
protocol (the E89 pairs set and the E90 set, cert
scorers verbatim)?

## Method

Session 2 re-ran both cert sets. Bar as specified: the
certificate files clean iff every axis is identical or
within 1 title of the restoration cert **and** eval sits
inside the ±2% de-noising band (the Mint de-noising
band finding; TUNING #236);
any axis ≥3 titles off is named drift, flagged
provisional to the main-chat review, which ruled here.

## Measured result

- Every structural axis reproduced **exactly** on both
  sets: E89 pairs 40/40, labels 40/40 + 40/40, forbidden
  0; E90 20/20 across. Eval in band on both (−0.5%,
  −1.3%). E90 hook −1, inside the ≤1 clause.
- The sole trip: E89 hook-form **+6** (cert 30/40 →
  36/40) — favorable direction, and the best E89 hook
  read on record (sittings 32 / 30 / 36). Mechanism,
  measured in-session: phi4-mini is **not
  seed-deterministic** at temp 0.3 / seed 42 on this
  lane (a discarded warm vs its own scored draw differ
  2/5 titles on identical settings; byte-identity vs
  cert 3/12 batches) — the hook axis rides near-tie
  punctuation variants while the structural axes are
  draw-invariant.

## Verdict

**CERTIFIED.** The certificate is filed with the hook
axis annotated as a **range (30–36)** on a
non-deterministic lane, not a point value. The ruling
adopts the runner's amendment with it: no byte-identity
expectation on this lane, and hook-class axes are read
as ranges. The provisional flag is cleared.
